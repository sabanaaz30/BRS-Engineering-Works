About B.R.S. Engineering Works

Established in 2006, B.R.S. Engineering Works is a trusted manufacturer and repairer of agricultural implements based in Davanagere, Karnataka.

We manufacture durable, high-quality agricultural implements and provide reliable repair and fabrication services to support farmers with efficient farming solutions.
